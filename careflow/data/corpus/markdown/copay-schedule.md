# Co-payment and Cost-Share Schedule

## MERIDIAN HEALTH PARTNERS - OPERATIONS POLICY

---

### 1.0. POLICY TITLE

1.1. Cost-Share Schedule: Meridian Employee Health Plans

### 2.0. POLICY NUMBER

2.1. MHP-FIN-CS-003-V1.2

### 3.0. EFFECTIVE DATE

3.1. January 1, 2024

### 4.0. PURPOSE

4.1. To define the specific patient cost-sharing responsibilities for eligible medical services across the four Meridian Health Partners internal health plans: Meridian Core, Meridian Enhanced, Meridian Premium, and Meridian HSA Advantage.
4.2. To provide clear parameters for annual deductibles, coinsurance rates, out-of-pocket maximums, and specific service co-pays.

### 5.0. SCOPE

5.1. This policy applies to all Meridian Health Partners employees and their enrolled dependents participating in one of the four specified Meridian Health Plans.
5.2. All figures represent in-network benefits. Out-of-network benefits are subject to separate, higher cost-sharing provisions not detailed in this document.

### 6.0. GENERAL PROVISIONS

6.1. **Plan Year:** The plan year for all Meridian Health Plans commences on January 1 and concludes on December 31 of each calendar year. All deductibles and out-of-pocket maximums reset on January 1 annually.
6.2. **Out-of-Pocket Maximum (OOPM) Inclusions:** All patient-paid deductibles, coinsurance, and co-pays for covered services accumulate towards the annual Out-of-Pocket Maximum. Once the OOPM is reached, the plan pays 100% of covered in-network services for the remainder of the plan year.

### 7.0. COST-SHARE SCHEDULE: MERIDIAN EMPLOYEE HEALTH PLANS

7.1. **Plan Summary Table:** The following table outlines the key cost-share parameters for each Meridian Health Plan.

| Feature                    | Meridian Core | Meridian Enhanced | Meridian Premium | Meridian HSA Advantage |
| :------------------------- | :------------ | :---------------- | :--------------- | :--------------------- |
| **Annual Deductible**      | $2,000        | $1,000            | $500             | $4,000                 |
| **Coinsurance Rate**       | 20%           | 10%               | 5%               | 15%                    |
| **Out-of-Pocket Maximum**  | $7,000        | $5,000            | $3,000           | $8,500                 |
| **Specialist Visit Co-pay**| $60           | $40               | $25              | $75 (1)                |
| **Imaging Co-pay**         | $150          | $100              | $50              | $200 (1)               |
| **Lab Work Co-pay**        | $30           | $20               | $10              | $40 (1)                |

*(1) For the Meridian HSA Advantage Plan, co-pays for Specialist Visits, Imaging, and Lab Work apply only after the annual deductible has been fully satisfied. Prior to deductible satisfaction, all charges for these services apply directly to the deductible.*

7.2. **Specific Co-pay Definitions:**

7.2.1. **Specialist Visit Co-pay:** A fixed fee paid by the patient for an office visit to a specialist physician (e.g., cardiologist, dermatologist, orthopedist). This co-pay is distinct from charges for procedures performed during the visit.
7.2.2. **Imaging Co-pay:** A fixed fee paid by the patient for covered diagnostic imaging services, including but not limited to X-rays, MRIs, CT scans, and ultrasounds.
7.2.3. **Lab Work Co-pay:** A fixed fee paid by the patient for covered diagnostic laboratory services.

7.3. **Telehealth Visit Co-pay:**

7.3.1. Telehealth visits for specialist consultations carry a co-pay of exactly 40 percent of the respective in-person specialist co-pay for each plan.
7.3.2. **Meridian Core Telehealth Co-pay:** $24.00 ($60.00 x 0.40)
7.3.3. **Meridian Enhanced Telehealth Co-pay:** $16.00 ($40.00 x 0.40)
7.3.4. **Meridian Premium Telehealth Co-pay:** $10.00 ($25.00 x 0.40)
7.3.5. **Meridian HSA Advantage Telehealth Co-pay:** $30.00 ($75.00 x 0.40)

### 8.0. PATIENT SHARE CALCULATION EXAMPLE (Deductible Partially Met)

8.1. **Scenario Parameters:**
    8.1.1. **Plan:** Meridian Core
    8.1.2. **Annual Deductible:** $2,000
    8.1.3. **Coinsurance Rate:** 20%
    8.1.4. **Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $7,000
    8.1.5. **Patient's Deductible Met To Date:** $1,200
    8.1.6. **New Service:** Outpatient surgical procedure with an allowed charge of $3,500. (This service is subject to deductible and coinsurance).

8.2. **Calculation Steps:**
    8.2.1. **Determine Remaining Deductible:** The patient has met $1,200 of their $2,000 annual deductible.
        *   Remaining Deductible = $2,000 - $1,200 = $800.
    8.2.2. **Apply Charges to Deductible:** The first $800 of the $3,500 allowed charge for the outpatient procedure is applied to the remaining deductible.
        *   Patient pays $800 to satisfy the deductible.
    8.2.3. **Calculate Remaining Charge for Coinsurance:** After the deductible is met, the remaining portion of the allowed charge is subject to coinsurance.
        *   Remaining Charge = $3,500 (Allowed Charge) - $800 (Deductible Met) = $2,700.
    8.2.4. **Apply Coinsurance:** The patient is responsible for 20% coinsurance on the remaining $2,700.
        *   Coinsurance Amount = $2,700 x 0.20 = $540.
        *   Patient pays $540 in coinsurance.
    8.2.5. **Total Patient Share for This Service:**
        *   Total Patient Share = $800 (Deductible) + $540 (Coinsurance) = $1,340.
    8.2.6. **Out-of-Pocket Maximum Accumulation:** This $1,340 will be added to the patient's existing out-of-pocket accumulation, moving them closer to their $7,000 OOPM.
